Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what personal information Lost & Found (the "Platform") collects, how it is used, who can see it, and how long it is kept. We handle personal information in line with the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173) of the Philippines.

1. Information we collect

  • Account details: first and last name, email address, and password. Passwords are stored only in a scrambled (hashed) form that no one, including the admin, can read.
  • Valid ID or student ID photo: uploaded at sign up so the admin can confirm who you are before approving your account.
  • Profile details: mobile number, address, and an optional profile photo.
  • Reports and requests: item names, descriptions, photos, dates, locations, contact numbers, claim requests, found notices, proof photos, and comments.
  • Activity records: actions such as logging in, submitting, approving, and deleting, with the date and time, to keep the Platform accountable.

2. Sign in with Google

If you choose "Continue with Google," Google shares only your name, email address, and profile picture with the Platform. We use your email to find your account or fill in the sign up form. We don't get your Google password, contacts, files, or any other Google data, and we don't post anything on your behalf.

3. How we use your information

  • To create and verify accounts, and to let you log in.
  • To show, match, and return lost and found items.
  • To send account emails: password reset codes, temporary passwords, and notices when your password is changed or your account is deleted.
  • To keep the Platform safe and prevent false claims.

We don't sell your information, show ads, or use it for anything unrelated to the Platform.

4. Who can see your information

  • Admin: all account details, including your valid ID, to verify and manage accounts.
  • Staff: reports, requests, and account details needed to review them. Staff can't see valid ID photos.
  • Other users: your name and profile photo, reports after staff approve and post them, and the requests sent for your own reports. Other users can't see your valid ID, email, address, or password.

We share information outside the Platform only when required by law.

5. Where your information is stored

Information is stored in a database on our web host (InfinityFree). Emails are sent through Gmail. Pages load fonts from Google Fonts, and "Continue with Google" loads Google's sign-in service.

6. How long we keep it

  • Your information is kept while your account exists.
  • When an account is deleted, its reports and history are kept for 90 days so you can bring them back if you sign up again with the same email. After 90 days, they are erased.
  • Password reset codes and temporary passwords expire after 1 minute 30 seconds.

7. Your rights

Under the Data Privacy Act, you can ask to access, correct, or delete your personal information. You can update your profile and valid ID in Account Settings, or delete your account there at any time. For anything else, email us.

8. Contact

Questions or requests about your privacy: minicapstonproject@gmail.com
