Delete your account?

You'll be logged out and can't use this account anymore. Deleting it also removes:

  • Your profile and photo
  • Your lost and found reports
  • Your claim requests and found notices

We keep a copy for days. If you sign up again with the same email, you can bring it back or start fresh.
